How to read them

  • Two states per colour. Resting is a softened version, hover is the full colour, drag is one step darker. The track stays light grey (--c-100) in every option.
  • Dark green is the branded default: clearly ours, still quiet at rest.
  • Light green and Leaf are friendlier but sit close to the green used for chips and active states, so the bar competes a little with real content.
  • Dark grey is the most neutral and never fights the page; it also reads as the most "system default".
  • Light grey is the quietest. It is a step darker than the track so it stays visible, but on a busy list it can get lost.
  • Stone is the warm-neutral twin of dark grey, tuned to the cream canvas rather than the cool one.
  • Ink uses green-950, the near-black already used for scrims. Premium and very calm.
  • Grey → green is my pick: neutral while you are reading, brand green the moment you reach for it. Quiet by default, unmistakably ours on interaction.
